If Dhurandhar was technically brilliant propaganda that at least tried to hide what it was doing, Dhurandhar 2 just goes full mask-off. This is propaganda on steroids. No kidding. No finesse, no subtlety, just straight-up chest-thumping, BJP cheerleading cinema. The film literally opens with India will decide Pakistan's future blah blah and somehow gets more unhinged from there. By the end, your hero isn't just fighting terrorists, he's influencing Pakistarni elections, justifying demonetisation, and basically acting like a one-man foreign policy making trips to Dubai ffs. Muslims are not characters. Never. Just ugly aesthetics. Either terrorists, corrupt politicians, or brainless mobs screaming Allahu Akbar, Sar Tan Se Juda while doing horrific shit. There's zero attempt to humanise anyone on that side. Indian victims get grief, faces, emotion. Pakistani bodies get gore. That contrast isn't accidental. And the politics... my god. It's not even coded anymore. It's outright glazing the current regime. Dissenters are hinted to be traitors, NGOS are shady, Khalistanis thrown in for extra spice. At one point genuinely expected a thanks to Modi ji end credit. No shame. Why even make movies, Dhar??? Start contesting in elections now, please. What's worse is how it's packaged. Slick action, loud BGM, slow-mo violence designed to make you feel powerful. The theatre was in? People were clapping at literal dismemberment. Laughing when Muslim characters got brutalised. That's not just cinema. That's conditioning. Also, it's just... exhausting. 4 hours of blood, noise, and coincidences that magically favour the hero. Even the technical edge from Part 1 is gone, VEX looks off, music doesn't land, and the whole thing feels like a bloated web series pretending to be a movie. If Part 1 blurred the line between storytelling and propaganda, Part 2 erases it completely.
This isn't cinema trying to explore conflict. It's cinema trying to simplify it into us good, them evil and sell that anger back to you as patriotism. And that's the most dangerous part. I definitely was ready for this (part 1 was the tell). Holy fuck.
